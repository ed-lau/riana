# LVE vs ATR cardiac D₂O turnover — clean v1.1.0 reintegrate + cross-tissue Δk

- **Date:** 2026-06-28
- **Branch:** `1.1.0`
- **Status:** **Reference run.** Clean reintegrate of the mouse cardiac D₂O time series on current
  defaults + `deberneh_2025_rss`, curated at **depth ≥ 6 / R² > 0.8 / Spep ≥ 6**, rolled up to a
  cross-tissue Δk contrast. 343 proteins differ in turnover between atrium and left ventricle.
- **Inputs:** `runs/lve_atr_clean` — SDRF `data/timeseries_lve_atr` (quantms DDA mzTab), 24 runs =
  **2 tissues × 12 timepoints** (LVE = left ventricle → condition `control`; ATR = atrium →
  `atrium`), 0–30 days, single biological replicate/timepoint, RIA 0.046 (from SDRF).
- **Pipeline:** `integrate` (iso0–5, ±10 ppm, apex window 0.15 min, q ≤ 0.01) → `fit --coefficients
  deberneh_2025_rss --label hw --depth 6` → `rollup --model "linear simple" --reference-condition
  control --min-r2 0.8 --method weighted --parsimony unique` (Spep ≥ 6 pre-gate).

## TL;DR

- **Atrium turns over ~1.24× faster than left ventricle** (median peptidoform k_deg **0.074 vs
  0.060 /day**) — the same direction and magnitude as the pre-1.0.0 LVE-ATR result, now reproduced
  on the current integrator + the Deberneh LC-MS labelling-site table.
- **343 / 491 cross-tissue-quantified proteins differ significantly** (Benjamini-Hochberg
  `p_adj < 0.05`): **289 faster in atrium, 54 faster in ventricle.**
- **Atrium-faster** is led by **secreted / acute-phase and matrix-remodelling** proteins — complement
  **C3**, serpins **Serpina1e/1b**, the collagen chaperone **Serpinh1** (HSP47), transferrin **Tf**,
  and cardiac troponin T **Tnnt2** (19 peptides). **Ventricle-faster** is led by **chaperone /
  proteostasis** proteins — the myosin chaperone **Unc45b**, chaperonins **Cct6a/Cct2**, **Hk2**.
- Clean extraction throughout: 21,747 peptidoforms / 2,728 proteins identified, **no integrate
  warnings**, median curated R² **0.901**.

## Notes & caveats

- **Replication is across timepoints (animals), not peptides — verified in code.** The `linear
  simple` rollup inverse-variance collapses a protein's peptides to **one θ per (biorep, timepoint)**
  (`_collapse_group` / `_weighted_theta`, σ from the M5 prediction-interval width `(hi−lo)/3.29`)
  *before* the φ-space OLS (`fit_linear_deltak`: `φ ~ 0 + day:C(condition)`). So Δk SE/p come from the
  OLS residual scatter across the per-timepoint points — and as each timepoint is a distinct animal,
  that is genuine biological + technical variance, not peptide pseudoreplication. The regression's
  replication is **`n_points`** (median 11 of 12 timepoints/condition), **not `n_peptides`**: e.g.
  Hnrnpu rests on 9 + 9 timepoints despite 2 peptides. (`n_peptides` sets each collapsed point's
  precision, not the df.)
- **Real residual caveats:** (1) the φ-OLS is *ordinary*, not weighted by each timepoint's θ-precision,
  so peptide-deep timepoints aren't up-weighted (it is homoscedastic over heteroscedastic φ points);
  (2) **plateau truncation thins `n_points` for fast proteins** — C3 (k_ATR 0.93/day) keeps only 4
  atrium points past the φ-limit — so the fastest hits have the shortest curves and are the weakest by
  replication (read C3 as a lead). Deeply-sampled hits (Tnnt2, Tf: 8–10 points/condition) are robust.
- **Direction is biologically coherent:** secreted acute-phase/serpin and ECM-remodelling proteins
  (C3, Serpina1e/1b, Serpinh1, Tf) turn over faster in atrium; chaperone/proteostasis machinery
  (Unc45b, Cct2/6a) faster in ventricle — consistent with the chamber's contractile/secretory load
  difference. Not over-interpreted here beyond the ranking.
- **Reproduces the prior result** (`runs/lve_atr`, pre-1.0.0: "atrium 1.24× faster"). The Deberneh
  table + current integrator do not move the headline; this run is the auditable v1.1.0 baseline.
- The integrate now emits the `iso{N}_obs_mz` / `dspacing` columns, so the **`fs_ds` mass-defect
  cross-check** is available on this dataset (`riana_fit_fractions.txt`) for a follow-up QC pass.
